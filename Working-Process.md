
# Working Process

1. The soil moisture sensor is inserted into the soil.
2. The sensor detects the moisture level of the soil.
3. The ESP32 receives the sensor data.
4. The ESP32 processes the moisture readings.
5. When the soil becomes dry, the relay turns ON.
6. The water pump starts supplying water.
7. When the soil gets enough moisture, the pump turns OFF.

# Advantages

- Saves water.
- Reduces manual work.
- Automatic irrigation.
- Helps in plant care.
- Low-cost IoT project.

# Applications

- Home gardens.
- Agriculture fields.
- Greenhouses.
- Smart farming.
